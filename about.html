Your local ARB specialists in Warwick

Locally owned, based on Ogilvie Road, and set up to handle the whole job.

Southern Downs 4x4 is a locally owned 4WD accessories business based in Warwick. We supply and fit ARB and other proven brands, and we do the fitting ourselves, in our own workshop.

We're not a mechanical workshop and we don't run a parts counter for its own sake. Our job is simple: work out what your vehicle needs to do, recommend the right gear, and fit it properly.

Most of what comes through our doors is farm utes, tow rigs, tourers and work vehicles from around the Southern Downs. We see the same conditions our customers drive in, which makes it easier to recommend gear that actually holds up.
