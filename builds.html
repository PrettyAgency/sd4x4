Completed 4WD builds — Warwick and the Southern Downs

Real set-ups, planned and fitted in our own workshop.

We don't just sell accessories — we plan how everything works together, then fit it ourselves. Here's a look at the kind of builds we deliver. Real customer jobs are added as they're completed.

Tradie work and weekend rig

Canopy, alloy tray, driving lights and storage that works Monday to Friday and tows the boat on Saturday.

River-ready off-road tourer

Bar, recovery points and lighting set up for water crossings and rough forestry tracks.

Weekend fishing and towing rig

Roof rack, canopy and a set-up strong enough to tow the boat down and camp the night.

High-country touring build

Bar, roof rack and driving lights for long days exploring the ranges.

Drawers, fridge slide and an awning that turns the tray into a basecamp, with the protection and lighting to get there and back.
